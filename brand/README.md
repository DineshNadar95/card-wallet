# Card Wallet brand

- `logo-mark.svg`: the mark (two cards, the front one checked as "the one to use"). Used as the
  web favicon/header logo (`apps/web/public/`) and the app splash.
- `app-icon.svg`: full-bleed app icon (iOS masks the corners).
- `google-consent-logo-120.png`: for the Google OAuth consent screen, **only** if you go through
  Google brand verification (it requires home page, privacy policy and terms links).

Colors are the design tokens: accent `#0B6E73`, better-value green `#1F6B3A`, chip `#D9C08A`.
PNGs in `apps/` were rendered from these SVGs; re-render after editing them.
